# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in EveryO to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

* being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences,
* giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
* focusing on what is best for the project and its users,
* showing patience with newcomers — everyone was one once.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

* harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private,
* sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission,
* sustained disruption of discussions,
* other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions and code review — and when someone is representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the repository maintainers by opening an issue
or, when the matter is sensitive, by contacting a maintainer privately through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly as a small volunteer
project can manage, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
